## How to read it

- **✓** shipped, per the product's source below. **$** shipped in a paid tier only. **◐** partial, limited or third-party. **✗** stated absent, requested on the vendor's own board, or declined by the vendor. **·** not established in this pass. A dot is not proof of absence.
- **Ours** is the last column of both tables: **Shipped** (with the code or the user guide), **I-xx** (planned), **D-xx** (waits on an owner decision), **Missing** (no owner found), or **Non-goal**.
- Both tables share the same row ids. Table A holds deckbuilders and Commander data sites. Table B holds trackers, tournament software, stores, play clients and AI tools. "AI tools" in table B is EDHcheck AI, MTG Agents, Mana Grove, DeckCheck and Farseek taken together; the cell names which one.
