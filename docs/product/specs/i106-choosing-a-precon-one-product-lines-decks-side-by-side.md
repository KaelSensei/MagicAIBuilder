## Overview

"Which precon should I buy?" is one of r/EDH's recurring questions. "Which precon should I buy for playing without upgrades?" drew 115 points and 133 comments on 2026-03-26 [1]; "Which precon is best suited to start a landfall deck?" 40 points and 109 comments [1]; "Which Strixhaven Commander Precon Is Best Straight Out of the Box?" 39 points and 52 comments [1]; "Which precon is good for beginners" 35 points and 39 comments [1]. The people asking are new players, returning players and gift buyers, and they ask about one product line at a time: which of these four or five boxes.

The answers they get are articles. Wizards' "Reality Fracture Buyer's Guide for Commander" names a target player per deck and says the Foundations decks are "where to start", but rates no complexity and names no bracket [2]. EDHREC's "How To Buy a New Commander Precon" judges precons on ease of piloting, price, upgrade potential and power against Brackets 2 and 3, and points to no tool [3]. The tools that exist show numbers that do not answer the question: Draftsim's precon database (186 decks across 50 sets, prices updated 2026-11-06) shows singles value, sealed price and a deal ratio, and states that "Every precon is Bracket 2 (Core) out of the box" [4], which conflicts with Wizards' 2025-10-21 update that I-48 records; DeckCheck filters precons by a 1–10 power range [5], the kind of score the seventeenth pass rejected.

This initiative shows one product line's decks side by side on facts the product already stores or computes, each with its owner's label: colour identity, play styles, the pilot load band and its factors, the bracket floors the sealed list trips, the route line, singles value against sealed price once there is a price source, and the cost of the first upgrade bundle. It shows no power score, no win rate and no blanket bracket.

**Why a new initiative.** I-17 serves a player who owns or has chosen one precon; its two pre-purchase lines are for one precon. Comparing a line's decks is a different job with its own page, its own user (the buyer, often a gift buyer) and its own rule against a power score. The research note proposed it as a scope line on I-17 and called it "borderline new"; the eighteenth pass gives it a file because it is a distinct deliverable with its own surface. **Why Next.** Slices 1 to 3 read signals that Next initiatives already build and need nothing new; the evidence is an official buyer's guide, two shipped competitor tools and threads to 115 points.

## Functional requirements

### 1. Principles

1.1 Every column is another initiative's output, shown with that initiative's label (Exact, Estimated, Computed) and its date. I-106 computes no new figure.
1.2 No power score, no 1–10 scale, no win rate, no "best deck" and no blanket "every precon is Bracket 2". A floor the sealed list trips is shown as I-77 states it.
1.3 A signal that does not exist yet shows "not computed yet", never a blank that reads as zero.

### 2. The comparison

2.1 One page per product line in I-17's catalogue (for example the five Foundations Commander decks, or the four Star Trek decks): one column per deck, the face commander and the box art credit as I-17 shows them.
2.2 Rows, in order: colour identity; play styles ([I-54](i54-find-your-next-commander.md)); how it wins, one line per route ([I-65](i65-how-this-deck-wins.md)); pilot load band with its factors ([I-99](i99-a-pilot-sheet-and-how-heavy-this-deck-is-to-pilot.md)); bracket floors the sealed list trips ([I-77](i77-a-bracket-estimate-players-can-audit.md)); singles value against sealed price, both labelled with source and date ([I-32](i32-seller-handoff-and-affiliate-carts.md), [I-96](i96-two-labelled-prices-and-no-conversion.md)); the cost of I-17's first bundle.
2.3 Each deck links to its catalogue page, to I-17's "open in the playtest" and, signed in, to "Copy this deck".
2.4 A player may hide rows and focus one deck; the choice is kept on the device only.

### 3. Entry points

3.1 From a catalogue precon's page, "Compare with the other decks of this line".
3.2 From [I-39](i39-first-run-activation-and-measurement.md)'s first-run sample decks and from [I-54](i54-find-your-next-commander.md) 3.7's next steps.

## Data model / API

```
PreconLine        = I-17 catalogue grouping (productLineId, name, releasedAt)       -- no new table
ComparisonRow     colourIdentity | playStyles | routes | pilotLoad | bracketFloors | priceVsSealed | firstBundleCost
RowCell           deckId, value?, label: exact | estimated | computed | notComputedYet, owner (I-54 | I-65 | I-99 | I-77 | I-32 | I-17), asOf
PreconComparison  lineId, decks[], rows[]{row, cells[]}
```

- `comparePrecons(line, signals)` is a pure function over the catalogue entries and each owner's stored output; `ComparisonRow` is a discriminated union with an exhaustive `never` guard.
- Route: `GET /api/precons/lines/[lineId]/comparison`, cached per catalogue version and signal version. No new table and no write.

## Non-goals

- A power score, a 1–10 scale, a win rate, a "best deck" verdict or a blanket bracket for precons.
- Upgrading a precon one owns, its sealed diff, bundles and ledger: those stay [I-17](i17-precon-path-and-tiered-upgrades.md)'s.
- Comparing decks across product lines, or arbitrary decks: one line at a time, as buyers ask. Comparing a pod's decks is [I-15](i15-rule-zero-sheets-and-pod-profiles.md) slice 4's.
- Selling sealed product or an affiliate link on the page ([I-32](i32-seller-handoff-and-affiliate-carts.md) decides any handoff).
- Reviews or ratings of precons by players.

## Testing strategy

- **Read model (solitary):** a line of five decks yields five columns in catalogue order; a missing signal yields `notComputedYet` with its owner; a row never carries a value without a label and a date; an unknown row kind fails to compile.
- **No score (solitary):** the comparison's output type has no numeric power field; a test fails if a row named "power" or "score" is added.
- **Page (sociable):** signed out, the page renders for the Foundations line from fixtures; "Copy this deck" appears only signed in; hiding a row keeps it hidden after reload on the same device.
- **Accessibility and layout:** the table has a caption and row headers; at 360 px each deck becomes a card with the same rows in the same order.
- **Red first:** each slice starts with one failing test the PR records, as [`docs/engineering/tdd-with-ai.md`](../../engineering/tdd-with-ai.md) requires. Slice 1's first Red: a line of five decks with no play-style output returns five `playStyles` cells labelled `notComputedYet`, owner I-54.

## Feature contract (Full, 2026-11-19)

The roadmap entry is a link. This section is the player contract. The engineering slices above stay the build plan.

### Summary and problem

A new player, a returning player or a gift buyer choosing between the four or five decks of one precon line gets articles, singles values or a 1–10 power score. Nothing shows the decks side by side on how heavy each is to pilot, which bracket floors its list trips, how it wins and what a first upgrade costs, with each figure's source.

### Goals

- A signed-out visitor opens the Foundations Commander line and sees five decks side by side: colour identity, play styles, how each wins, its pilot load band with factors, the bracket floors its sealed list trips, singles value against sealed price with their dates, and the first bundle's cost, each labelled with its owner and date.

### Non-goals

- A power score, a win rate, a "best deck" or a blanket bracket; upgrading an owned precon (I-17); comparing across lines or arbitrary decks; selling sealed product; player reviews.

### Personas

The casual pod player buying a first or next precon, often for someone else; the collector and budget player weighing singles value against the box. See the roadmap persona table.

### User stories

#### US-106-01

As a new player choosing between a line's decks, I want them side by side on how each plays and how heavy each is to pilot, so that I buy the one that fits before paying $29.99 to $49.99.

#### US-106-02

As someone buying a precon as a gift, I want each deck's facts with their source, so that I can choose without knowing the game well.

#### US-106-03

As a bracket-focused player, I want the bracket floors each sealed list trips, not a blanket "Bracket 2", so that I know which box fits my table out of the box.

#### US-106-04

As a signed-out visitor, I want the whole comparison without an account, so that I can decide before I sign up.

#### US-106-05

As a player using a keyboard or a screen reader, I want the comparison as a table with headers, so that I can read a row across decks.

### UX flow and states

A catalogue precon's page links "Compare with the other decks of this line". The comparison is a table on wide screens and a stack of cards at 360 px, with a row filter and "Focus this deck".

- Empty: a line with one deck shows that deck and says there is nothing to compare.
- Loading: a busy state that keeps the table still.
- Error: per row, "not available right now" with a retry.
- Partial: "not computed yet" cells, each naming its owner.

- **Thin signals at launch.** Most rows say "not computed yet" until their owners ship. Mitigation: slice 2 ships only once colour identity and floors exist, and the page says which rows are coming.

**User story.** As a new player choosing between a line's decks, I want them side by side on how each plays, so that I buy the one that fits.

**Acceptance.**

- Five Foundations decks show in catalogue order, every cell labelled and dated.
- A missing signal says "not computed yet" and names its owner.
- No row is a power score, a win rate or "Bracket 2 out of the box".
- "Copy this deck" appears only signed in.

**UX.** A table on wide screens, cards at 360 px; a row filter and "Focus this deck".

**Data and API.** No new table; `GET /api/precons/lines/[lineId]/comparison`.

**i18n and access.** English and French chrome; card names stay the printing's language. A captioned table with headers.

**Telemetry.** `precon_comparison_viewed {lineDecks, rowsHidden}`, `precon_comparison_copy {}`. Reserved for I-39.

**Rollout.** `site.preconComparison`, on once the first line is loaded.

**Open questions.** Which lines first; singles value without a ratio; quoting the buyer's guide.

### Definition of done

A signed-out visitor opens the Foundations Commander line, sees five decks side by side with colour identity, play styles, routes, pilot load, bracket floors, singles value against sealed price and the first bundle's cost, each labelled with its owner and date, sees "not computed yet" where a signal is missing, finds no power score anywhere, and, after signing in, copies one deck.
