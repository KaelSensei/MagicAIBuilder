This register lists every open decision that only the owner can make. It holds 87 decisions, merged from about 300 raw mentions across those sources (299 by this register's own tally). D-78 and D-79 were added on 2026-11-10 from the ninth pass's gap sweep, and D-80 to D-87 the same day from the tenth pass's specs, I-72 to I-75.

How it was built:

- **Owner decisions only.** Questions for the lawyer or the accountant are in a separate document. Where an owner decision cannot be final before a legal answer, the entry says "waits on legal" and names the area.
- **One entry per decision.** When several specs ask the same thing, the entry merges them and lists every initiative it blocks.
- **Recommendations are quoted.** "Specs recommend" quotes the spec that makes the recommendation or proposal. "No recommendation in the specs" means none was found.
- **Slice numbers are the specs' own.** Links point to headings, never to line numbers, because the roadmap changes daily.
- **Nothing is dropped silently.** Questions the specs leave to the integrator, to measurement or to outside research are listed at the end.

### D-01: AI allowance numbers

- **Question:** what monthly AI allowance and spend ceiling does production start with, and does a deck that the card-data check rejects use up a build?
- **Options:** keep the branch defaults of 10 builds and 30 suggestion calls per user a month and a 20 USD global monthly ceiling; set other numbers through the environment variables the branch reads; for a rejected deck, keep the unit consumed or give it back.
- **Specs recommend:** "free 10 builds and 30 suggestion calls a month; paid 100 and 300" ([I-30 requirement 4.3](specs/i30-business-model-and-paid-tier.md#4-ai-metering-and-quotas-production-safety-ships-before-billing)). "Calls that fail on our side (truncated, parse failure, validator rejection, provider error) do not consume the allowance" (4.4). No recommendation in the specs for the ceiling: the branch's own comment says "The ceiling is not in the spec".
- **Finding:** on `feat/ai-usage-metering-and-monthly-quota` (`5879b6d`), the card-data check of #1318 runs after the metered call has recorded `ok`. A deck it rejects, for example a commander that cannot lead a deck, therefore keeps its unit consumed. That differs from the spirit of requirement 4.4.
- **Unblocks:** merging the branch, which is I-30 slices 1 and 2 and I-02 requirement 7.2; it closes [I-05](roadmap.md#i-05-production-safety)'s quota scope and feeds I-66 requirement 6.4 and its AI alerts. I-30 slices 3 to 5 follow it.
- **Deadline:** before any public launch or promotion.
- **Cost of waiting:** on `dev` two routes call a model with no metering, and the only limits are per minute and per instance. AI spend has no ceiling. I-30's open question "How small can the free AI allowance be" stays a guess until real usage is metered.
- **Sources:** [I-66 open question 3](specs/i66-running-costs-and-margin.md#open-questions), [I-30 open questions](specs/i30-business-model-and-paid-tier.md#open-questions), the seventh-pass status note.

### D-09: Minimum password length

- **Question:** is the minimum password length 12 characters, or 15 for accounts that have no passkey and no two-step sign-in?
- **Options:** 12, which meets the CNIL's 50-bit case by computation with less friction; 15, which NIST requires for a password used alone and OWASP strongly recommends.
- **Specs recommend:** no recommendation in the specs. Requirement 5.1 is written with 12 ("12 to 128 characters"), and the open question asks whether password-only accounts need 15 ([I-61 open question 3](specs/i61-account-security.md#open-questions)).
- **Unblocks:** [I-61](roadmap.md#i-61-account-security-passkeys-two-step-sign-in-and-sessions) slice 5, which carries the 12–128 rule and fixes the 72-byte bcrypt truncation, then slices 6 and 7.
- **Deadline:** slice 5 "goes first" and needs nothing else.
- **Cost of waiting:** low per day. If the rule moves to 15 after slice 5 ships, players who already set 12 to 14 characters need their own rule.
