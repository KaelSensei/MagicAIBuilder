## What the player can do

Two public pages, reached from each other's tab bar and from nowhere else in the app yet.

**The Comprehensive Rules page** (`/rules/comprehensive`):

- **Read the official rules, one chapter at a time.** The contents on the left list the nine
  sections and their chapters ("903. Commander"); the chosen chapter shows every rule, subrule
  (indented) and example (indented, in italics). The glossary is the last entry of the contents.
  The page opens on chapter 100 when the link names no chapter or an unknown one.
- **Search the rules and the glossary.** The search box above the contents matches the typed
  text anywhere in a rule or a glossary definition, without regard to case. Results show the rule
  number (or the glossary term) and the text, rules first, then glossary entries, up to 25.
- **Know which edition is shown.** Under the title: "Effective as of August 7, 2026." in the
  reader's language and date format ("En vigueur depuis le 7 août 2026." in French).
- **Share a chapter or a search.** Both live in the URL (`?chapter=903`, `?q=commander`), so a
  link opens the same view; the page works without JavaScript.

**The Game Changers and banlist page** (`/rules/game-changers`):

- **Browse the two official Commander lists** behind two tabs: the Game Changers, each with a "GC"
  badge, and the cards banned in Commander, each with a "Banned" badge. A count shows beside each
  tab once its list has loaded. The cards come live from Scryfall.
- **Search a list by name.** The search field keeps the cards whose name contains the typed text,
  by their English name or the name printed in the reader's language, and says how many match and
  for what.
- **Page through a list**, 25 cards a page, with Previous and Next and "Page X / Y". The page
  number is in the URL (`?page=2`): a shared or reloaded link opens that page, and a page change
  brings the title back into view.
- **Zoom a card.** A click on a tile opens the card at display size with its name, type line and
  position in the page ("3 / 25"). The arrow keys and the side buttons step through the page's
  cards; Escape, the close button or a click around the card closes the zoom and gives focus back
  to the tile. A card Scryfall has no image for says "No image" instead of a broken picture.
- **See every printing.** "View all arts" in the zoom opens the card's printing selector with
  every paper printing, newest first.
- **Read it in French.** The tabs, messages and badges are translated; the card names and type
  lines show the French printing when Scryfall has one, English otherwise.

Both pages are read-only. There is no account, no deck and nothing to save.

written; the eight other catalogs keep key parity). The pager's labels are in `common.pagination`.

Both pages are public and read nothing about the visitor.

| Action                                  | Guest | Signed in | Owner | Moderator | API key scope                                                           |
| --------------------------------------- | ----- | --------- | ----- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Read the Comprehensive Rules page       | yes   | yes       | yes   | yes       | none: a server-rendered page                                            |
| Read the Game Changers and banlist page | yes   | yes       | yes   | yes       | none; the shared search cache is a public GET, rate-limited per address |

## Not yet

- **A current edition.** The served rules are effective 2026-08-07; Wizards linked the 2026-11-06
  edition when I-47 was researched (2026-11). Updating is a manual drop-in today (the checklist in
  the module README); [I-47](../product/specs/i47-rules-answers-for-this-deck.md) slice 1 plans an
  import script with a diff report.
- **A link to one rule.** Rule paragraphs have no anchor, so `?rule=903.9a` does not exist and
  search results do not link to the rule in its chapter (I-47, slice 2).
- **Rules in French.** Wizards publishes the Comprehensive Rules in English only; the page
  translates its own labels and the date, not the rules.
- **A date on the lists.** The Game Changers and banlist tabs show live Scryfall data with no
  "as of" line and no history of changes
  ([I-48](../product/specs/i48-official-changes-reach-every-deck.md)).
- **Landing on the last page** when a link names a page beyond it; today it lands on page 1 and
  rewrites the URL (pinned since RR-01).
- **Keyboard tabs.** The two list toggles are buttons, not a WAI-ARIA tablist; arrow keys do not
  move between them.
- **An address bar without `?page=1`** on the first load of the lists page (pinned since RR-01).
- **A browser test.** No Playwright spec drives either page; the sociable Vitest suite is the
  end-to-end check short of a browser.
