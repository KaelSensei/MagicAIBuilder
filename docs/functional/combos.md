> [src/modules/combos/README.md](../../src/modules/combos/README.md).
> **Glossary:** [CONTEXT.md, Combos](../../CONTEXT.md#combos).
> **Last checked against the code:** 2026-11-12, commit `764f22a5`.

## What the player can do

- **See which combos the deck holds.** The **Combos** panel sits in the deck builder's analysis
  panel, between the AI suggestions and the meta panel. It lists every combo
  [Commander Spellbook](https://commanderspellbook.com/) knows whose cards are all in the deck:
  the cards involved, the effects produced, what else the combo needs, Spellbook's step-by-step
  description when it has one, and a link to the combo's page on Spellbook.
  ([User guide](../product/user-guide.md#deck-statistics))
- **Spot the infinite lines first.** Combos whose effects read as infinite or game-winning carry a
  red ♾ marker and come first; the panel is headed "Infinite combos" when the deck holds one.
- **Know what the combo still needs.** Under "Prerequisites" the panel lists the generic pieces
  Spellbook requires beyond the named cards ("Also needs: Permanent castable for {C}"), the game
  states to reach first, and the mana the combo spends.
- **Read the bracket consequence.** Each infinite combo of exactly two cards counts toward the
  bracket estimate: the deck's bracket score is floored at Bracket 3 and a warning explains why.
  ([User guide](../product/user-guide.md#deck-statistics))
- **Know when the result was observed.** The credit line "Combo data from Commander Spellbook"
  names the date the result was last fetched.

The panel is read-only. It appears for every deck the builder opens, in any format; the bracket
consequence applies to Commander decks, the only format with brackets.

## Rules applied

| Rule, in plain words                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Enforced where                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The lookup runs once the deck holds at least 10 cards (commander, partner and main deck). Below that the panel says "Add at least 10 cards to detect combos".                                                           | Product. A list of a few cards rarely holds a combo, and a new deck changes on every click; waiting for 10 cards keeps the first edits from spending Spellbook calls on lists that cannot yet answer.                                                                                | Browser (`useCombos`); the panel's own gate counts rows in every zone, see Edge cases                                     |
| Only the commander, the partner and the main deck are sent. Sideboard and "Considering" cards are not: a combo you are only considering is not one the deck holds.                                                      | Product                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Browser (`useCombos`)                                                                                                     |
| The companion is not sent today. A companion begins the game outside it and can be put into the hand once (CR 702.139a), so it can take part in a combo; the decision is to include it, and B2-43 will.                 | CR 702.139a; owner decision OD-07                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Browser (`useCombos`), pinned as current behaviour                                                                        |
| Only combos the deck fully contains are shown. Combos the deck is one card away from are not listed.                                                                                                                    | Product; Spellbook's `included` bucket                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Server (`find-my-combos.ts`)                                                                                              |
| A combo reads as **infinite** when one of its Spellbook effects names an infinite loop, a game win or loss, or a board-wide result ("infinite", "unlimited", "win the game", "lose the game", "draw all", "kills all"). | Product heuristic. Spellbook's effects carry no infinite flag, so their names are matched.                                                                                                                                                                                           | Server (`spellbook-variant.ts`)                                                                                           |
| A **two-card combo**, for the bracket estimate, is an infinite combo that uses exactly two named cards. Generic extra pieces Spellbook lists do not stop it from counting.                                              | Product reading of Wizards' bracket text; roadmap I-77 reviews it                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Browser and server (`isTwoCardInfiniteCombo`, shared code)                                                                |
| One two-card combo or more floors the bracket estimate at Bracket 3, never higher: Brackets 1 and 2 allow no two-card combos, Bracket 3 allows none before turn six, Brackets 4 and 5 do not restrict them.             | Wizards, Commander Brackets update of 2025-10-21: [article](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025) and the official table in the [bracket guide](../product/guides/commander-deckbuilding-by-bracket.md#the-official-table) | Browser (`scoreBracket`, deck-analysis context). A list cannot show how early a combo comes up, so turn six is not judged |
| Wizards' "two-card combos" also covers game-enders and lockouts that are not infinite. The app counts the infinite ones only.                                                                                           | Same source; mismatch listed in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md#format-rules)                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Browser and server                                                                                                        |
| Two decks with the same cards, in any order, case or spacing, share one Spellbook answer. A result is reused for 6 hours on the server and for 1 hour in the browser.                                                   | Product. Spellbook adds combos over days, not minutes; sharing one call across every viewer of a list keeps the app a sparse client, as Spellbook asks                                                                                                                               | Server (cache) and browser (TanStack Query)                                                                               |
| The server makes at most 60 Spellbook calls per minute, and each visitor at most 30 lookups per minute.                                                                                                                 | Commander Spellbook API root: 80 requests per minute "should be a safe rate", clients should stay sparse and handle 429 (<https://backend.commanderspellbook.com/>, read 2026-11-12)                                                                                                 | Server (`lookup-policy.ts`, `spellbook-budget.ts`, `POST /api/combos`)                                                    |
| When the lookup could not run, the panel says so and never claims the deck has no combos.                                                                                                                               | Product. "No known combos" is a finding; a check that never ran must not be shown as one                                                                                                                                                                                             | Browser (`fetchCombosForDeck`, `CombosPanel`)                                                                             |
| Combo data is credited to Commander Spellbook, with the observation date and a link to each combo's page.                                                                                                               | Product; Spellbook attribution                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Browser (`CombosPanel`)                                                                                                   |

## States and messages

All keys live in the `deck` namespace, under `combos`, in `src/messages/<locale>/deck.json`
(English and French written; other locales may reuse the English text).

| State              | What the player sees                                                                                             | i18n keys                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Too few cards      | "Add at least 10 cards to detect combos"                                                                         | `deck.combos.minCards`                                                        |
| Loading            | A spinner and "Checking Commander Spellbook…"                                                                    | `deck.combos.checking`                                                        |
| Empty              | "No known combos detected", with the credit line                                                                 | `deck.combos.noKnown`, `deck.combos.credit`                                   |
| Found              | "{count} found", the "Infinite combos" heading when one is infinite, one card per combo                          | `deck.combos.found`, `deck.combos.infinite`                                   |
| Combo details      | "Prerequisites", "Also needs: {piece}", "Mana needed: {mana}", "Steps on Commander Spellbook"                    | `deck.combos.prerequisites`, `.otherPiece`, `.manaNeeded`, `.viewOnSpellbook` |
| Lookup unavailable | "Commander Spellbook could not be reached, so combos were not checked. Try again later."                         | `deck.combos.unavailable`                                                     |
| Credit             | "Combo data from Commander Spellbook", the link labelled with the observation date                               | `deck.combos.credit`, `deck.combos.observedLabel`                             |
| Bracket warning    | "N infinite 2-card combo(s) detected — Bracket 3 minimum: Brackets 1–2 allow none, Bracket 3 none before turn 6" | Not localised: built in `src/lib/deck/bracket.ts`                             |

## Not yet

- **Combos one card away.** Spellbook's "almost included" bucket is dropped; roadmap I-65 ("How it
  wins") would list what the deck is one card from.
- **A bracket estimate players can audit.** Which combos counted, and whether a generic third
  piece should disqualify a two-card line: roadmap I-77.
- **Game-enders and lockouts** that are not infinite are not counted as two-card combos, though
  Wizards' text includes them.
- **The companion** in the lookup, and one ten-card threshold shared by the hook and the panel:
  B2-43.
- **A shared cache across server instances.** The cache and the Spellbook budget are per instance.
- **Combos on public deck pages.** The panel exists in the builder only.
