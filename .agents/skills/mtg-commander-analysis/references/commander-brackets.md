# Commander Bracket System (Updated February 2026)

The bracket system replaced the informal 1-10 power level scale. It provides a shared vocabulary for pregame conversations.

## Bracket Definitions

### Bracket 1: Exhibition

- **Purpose:** Casual, fun-first games. The most relaxed tier.
- **Characteristics:** Precons out of the box, theme decks, group hug, jank combos that rarely assemble
- **Power level:** Low synergy, cards chosen for flavor over efficiency
- **Game pace:** Games go long (10+ turns typical), board states get complex
- **Game Changers:** 0 allowed
- **Typical signals:** No tutors, no fast mana, no infinite combos, minimal interaction

### Bracket 2: Core

- **Purpose:** Upgraded precons, focused casual decks
- **Characteristics:** Strong synergy, good card quality, clear win conditions
- **Power level:** Medium. Decks have a plan and execute it, but not oppressively fast
- **Game pace:** Games last at least 6+ turns. Board development matters
- **Game Changers:** 0 allowed
- **Typical signals:** Some targeted removal, moderate card draw, no mass land destruction, no hard stax

### Bracket 3: Upgraded

- **Purpose:** Optimized casual. Strong decks that still prioritize fun multiplayer games
- **Characteristics:** Efficient card choices, strong synergies, haymaker plays
- **Power level:** Medium-high. Consistent, well-built decks
- **Game pace:** Games last at least 4+ turns. Decisive plays start around turns 5-7
- **Game Changers:** Up to 3 cards from the Game Changers list allowed
- **Typical signals:** Tutors (limited), efficient ramp, powerful engines, some free spells. No infinite combos intended, or combos require 3+ pieces

### Bracket 4: Optimized

- **Purpose:** High-power Commander. Near-cEDH or strong enough to hang with it
- **Characteristics:** Maximum efficiency, fast mana, cheap interaction, compact combos
- **Power level:** High. Decks are built to win, with redundancy and consistency
- **Game pace:** Games can end quickly (turn 4-6 wins possible)
- **Game Changers:** Unlimited
- **Typical signals:** Fast mana (Mana Crypt, Chrome Mox), efficient tutors, free counterspells, 2-card infinite combos

### cEDH (Beyond Bracket 4)

- **Not officially a bracket** but acknowledged as a distinct metagame
- **Purpose:** Competitive EDH, playing to win
- **Characteristics:** Optimized lists with extensive testing, meta-aware card choices
- **Game pace:** Games can end turns 2-4
- **Signals:** Full suite of fast mana, Ad Nauseam, Thoracle + Demonic Consultation, stax pieces

## Game Changers List

Game Changers are cards that dramatically warp games, particularly in early turns. The list is curated by WotC and updated periodically.

### Criteria for Game Changers

Cards that:

- Easily and dramatically warp Commander games
- Allow a player to run away with resources
- Shift games in ways many players find unpleasant
- Block opponents from playing the game
- Efficiently tutor for any card without meaningful downside
- Have outsized impact in early turns (WotC is moving away from high-CMC entries)

### Notable Game Changers Categories

**Fast Mana:**

- Sol Ring (staple but monitored)
- Mana Crypt
- Mana Vault
- Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond, Jeweled Lotus (banned as of late 2024/early 2025)

**Tutors:**

- Demonic Tutor
- Vampiric Tutor
- Imperial Seal
- Enlightened Tutor, Mystical Tutor

**Resource Engines:**

- Rhystic Study
- Smothering Tithe
- Cyclonic Rift
- Dockside Extortionist

**Free Interaction:**

- Force of Will
- Fierce Guardianship
- Deflecting Swat

**Stax/Lockdown:**

- Winter Orb
- Stasis
- Opposition Agent

> **Important:** The exact Game Changers list is updated periodically by WotC. Always verify the current list at: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/ (search for "Commander Brackets Beta Update")

## How Brackets Affect Deck Analysis

When analyzing a deck for a target bracket:

1. **Count Game Changers** - Does the deck exceed the bracket's limit?
2. **Assess win speed** - Can the deck win faster than the bracket's expected pace?
3. **Check combo density** - Bracket 1-2: no infinites. Bracket 3: 3+ piece combos OK. Bracket 4: anything goes
4. **Evaluate interaction density** - Higher brackets expect more and cheaper interaction
5. **Review mana efficiency** - Fast mana pushes decks up brackets regardless of other factors
6. **Consider social contract** - Brackets 1-2 heavily weight "fun for the table" factor

## Bracket Assessment Heuristic

Score these dimensions 1-4, average = estimated bracket:

| Dimension    | 1 (Exhibition)         | 2 (Core)                   | 3 (Upgraded)                    | 4 (Optimized)               |
| ------------ | ---------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Ramp**     | 5-6 pieces, all 3+ CMC | 8-10 pieces, some 2 CMC    | 10-12 pieces, signets/talismans | 12+, fast mana included     |
| **Draw**     | 3-5 pieces             | 6-8 pieces                 | 8-10 pieces                     | 10+, includes free draw     |
| **Removal**  | 3-5 targeted           | 6-8, mix of targeted+board | 8-10, efficient answers         | 10+, free/cheap interaction |
| **Tutors**   | 0                      | 0-1, narrow                | 2-3, some unrestricted          | 4+, Demonic/Vampiric etc.   |
| **Win cons** | Incidental/combat      | Focused synergy            | Powerful engines + backup       | Compact combos + redundancy |
| **Avg CMC**  | 3.5+                   | 3.0-3.5                    | 2.5-3.0                         | 2.0-2.5                     |
